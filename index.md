<section class="section" aria-labelledby="principles-title"><div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">Small enough to understand</p><h2 id="principles-title">Every mechanic earns its place.</h2><p>Ketch keeps spawning, movement, physics, scoring, feedback, difficulty, and reset behavior together in one readable SpriteKit scene.</p></div><div class="principle-grid"><article class="principle-card"><span class="card-number">01</span><h3>Move simply</h3><p>Tap horizontally and the basket moves smoothly while staying clamped inside the visible play area.</p></article><article class="principle-card"><span class="card-number">02</span><h3>Read every result</h3><p>Catches sparkle and bounce; misses shake the scene, pulse a heart, and visibly reduce the remaining lives.</p></article><article class="principle-card"><span class="card-number">03</span><h3>Grow the challenge</h3><p>Every five catches advances the level, increases gravity, and shortens the spawn delay.</p></article></div></section>
